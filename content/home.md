## About

I am a Ph.D. scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Madras, advised by [Dr. Puduru Viswanadha Reddy](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/vishwa/). My research is on inverse problems and credible incentive strategies for different classes of differential games.

- Inverse Differential Games with Descriptor Dynamics
- Credible Incentive Equilibrium Strategies in Static and Dynamic Network Games
- Optimal Control
- Game Theory & Optimization
